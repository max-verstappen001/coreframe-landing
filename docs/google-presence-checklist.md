## Google Search Console

- Confirm a **Domain property** for `coreframetech.in` (preferred) or verify both HTTPS URL-prefix variants if DNS access is unavailable.
- Check that all known owners and users are authorized; remove obsolete access.
- Submit `https://coreframetech.in/sitemap.xml` after deployment and verify that it is fetched successfully.
- Inspect the home page and the three priority service URLs:
  - `https://coreframetech.in/`
  - `https://coreframetech.in/capabilities/private-ai`
  - `https://coreframetech.in/capabilities/edge-ai`
  - `https://coreframetech.in/capabilities/ai-infrastructure`
- Confirm that Google-selected canonicals match the declared apex HTTPS URLs. Investigate any `www`, HTTP, trailing-slash or deployment-preview variants that Google selects instead.
- Request indexing for the changed home page and priority service pages where appropriate. Indexing requests are not guarantees and are unnecessary for every minor edit.
- Review Page indexing, HTTPS and Core Web Vitals reports after deployment. Track branded queries such as `Coreframe Technologies` separately from non-branded private-AI, on-premise-AI, edge-AI and LLMOps queries.
- Compare branded impressions, clicks, average position and the pages Google chooses over rolling 28-day and 3-month windows. Annotate the deployment date before judging impact.
